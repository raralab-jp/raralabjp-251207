Winza Sapphire 0.370ct “Tessellation 27 (B)”

This sapphire from Winza, Tanzania, was faceted in Arya Akhavan’s “Tessellation 27 (B)” design. It has a rich purple color, revealing a range of tones from reddish purple to bluish purple as the viewing angle changes. The geometric facets radiating from the center are a distinctive feature of this design. Larger facets on the crown offer a clear view of the inclusions and other internal features that give this stone its individual character. It has been heated.

When viewed from the crown side under 10× magnification, widespread bubble-like inclusions can be observed. A crack near the girdle is also visible to the unaided eye from the crown side.

A misalignment at the girdle on the pavilion side can be observed under 10× magnification.
